Say “Solana”.
Your phone answers.

Lost your phone somewhere nearby? Just say “Solana”. It rings loudly, vibrates, flashes and shows I'M HERE! until you find it.

Works offlineNo accountNo ads or trackingVoice stays on your phone

🎙️ Hands-free

Listens for “Solana”, even with the screen off and the phone locked.

🔔 Hard to miss

Loud alarm tones that get gradually louder, plus vibration, flashlight and a spoken “I'm here!”.

🔒 Private by design

Speech is recognised on the phone. Nothing is recorded or uploaded, and the app has no internet access.

🔋 Battery-aware

The speech engine sleeps while the room is quiet, and the alarm can stop itself after 5 or 10 minutes.

How to use it

  1. Open the app and allow the microphone and notifications.
  2. Turn listening on. Press Test Alarm once to check it on your phone.
  3. Can't find your phone? Say “Solana”.
